# Statistical Claim Limits

Legitimate claims are counts, classifiable-case proportions, feature-level outcomes, converter-specific observations, and cross-converter differences for this controlled benchmark. The 26 rows are not independent random samples from a population; no significance tests or population-wide estimates are justified. Aggregate rates are descriptive and secondary to feature-level reporting.

The study cannot claim that document conversion generally destroys accessibility, that one converter is globally better, or that results generalize to all versions/documents.
