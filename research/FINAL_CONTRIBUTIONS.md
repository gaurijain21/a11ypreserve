# Final Contributions

1. A source-grounded method for measuring whether known accessibility information survives document conversion.
2. A controlled benchmark of 13 verified DOCX accessibility fixtures with explicit machine-readable ground truth.
3. `a11ydiff`, an automated comparison framework relating source accessibility properties to converted PDF structure with retained evidence.
4. An empirical study of 26 real outputs from two DOCX-to-PDF pipelines, showing preservation, partial preservation, alteration, and confirmed loss across tested properties.

These statements are deliberately scoped and do not claim a universal converter ranking or a first-of-its-kind result.
