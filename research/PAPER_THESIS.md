# Paper Thesis Candidates

1. Accessible source documents do not necessarily retain all of their accessibility information during conversion, and source-grounded comparison can reveal changes that destination-only assessment cannot reconstruct.
2. A controlled source-to-destination evidence chain makes accessibility-information preservation measurable across real document-conversion pipelines.
3. In a 13-fixture DOCX-to-PDF benchmark, preservation outcomes were feature-specific and differed across the tested LibreOffice and Google Docs pipelines.
4. Tagged destination PDFs can remain structurally accessible while failing to preserve particular author-provided accessibility properties.
5. A11yPreserve demonstrates a practical way to test what accessibility information survives conversion without treating a destination validator as the preservation oracle.
