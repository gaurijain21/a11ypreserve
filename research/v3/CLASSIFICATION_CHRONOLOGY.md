# Classification chronology and post-hoc taxonomy disclosure

## Chronology

The original V1 categories (`PRESERVED`, `PARTIALLY_PRESERVED`, `ALTERED`, `LOST`, and `MEASUREMENT_ERROR`) were assigned before the later parser-triangulation and equivalence review. The source files, PDF outputs, manifests, hashes, and V1 primary classifications were then frozen.

The later evidence-aware review did not rerun or alter the experiment. It introduced a reporting overlay that distinguishes direct destination differences from cases in which a feature-specific equivalent representation could not be established. The V2 evidence-aware taxonomy therefore contains `VERIFIED_PRESERVED`, `OBSERVED_PARTIAL`, `ALTERED`, `CONFIRMED_LOST`, `UNRESOLVED_EQUIVALENCE`, and `MEASUREMENT_ERROR`. V1 remains archived for provenance; V2 is the primary evidence-aware interpretation.

This chronology is post-hoc with respect to the original conversion outputs. The paper must not say that the six-way evidence-aware labels, or the final uncertainty rules, were fixed before the V1 results were inspected. V3 makes the rule specification explicit and applies it prospectively to evidence records, but it cannot retroactively make the V2 taxonomy pre-registered.

## Frozen boundary

The V2 interpretation overlay does not change any DOCX fixture, PDF, manifest, hash, source contract, primary denominator, or historical classification. A disagreement between the V3 decision procedure and a V2 label is an audit finding; it is not a license to edit V2 evidence.

## Required manuscript wording

Use: “The original five-way labels were frozen before the later evidence/equivalence review. The six-way evidence-aware taxonomy was then applied as a documented post-hoc interpretive overlay; the underlying files and historical labels were unchanged.”

Avoid: “The feature contracts and classification rules were fixed before the final conversion results were reviewed.” That sentence is too strong for the evidence-aware rules.
