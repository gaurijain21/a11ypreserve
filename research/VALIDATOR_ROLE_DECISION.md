# Validator Role Decision

## Decision: FUTURE WORK

The project did not collect a systematic PAC, Acrobat Accessibility Checker, or veraPDF dataset for all 26 outputs with stable versions and settings. Validator observations may be useful exploratory context, but they are not complete enough for a core research question. The core paper therefore stands on source-grounded extraction and comparison; a future validator study can ask which preservation changes destination-only tools surface.
