# Final Decision: GO WITH SCOPE CHANGES

## What is killed

- The claim that the project is the first or only accessibility-after-conversion benchmark.
- A broad “all document formats and converters” program.
- A PDF→HTML checker/remediation system as the primary contribution.
- Any conclusion that an empty parser field proves semantic loss.

## What continues

Continue as a provenance-aware differential testing method for controlled authored fixtures. The primary result should be a per-feature source/output evidence record with the nine explicit states, a destination capability map, and conservative measurement-error handling.

## Required continuation gate

Before a paper or strong empirical claim, complete:

1. At least two independent conversion engines for the same edge.
2. Two source families and a small, pre-registered fixture matrix.
3. A PDF/tag-tree adapter or independent manual/object-level confirmation for PDF claims.
4. One negative control and one known-loss control.
5. Manual/assistive-technology verification of a stratified subset.
6. Reproducible version, configuration, hash and artifact records.

## Kill criteria

Kill the narrowed project if the continuation gate shows no measurable engine differences, if independent checks cannot validate source intent, or if its outputs are indistinguishable from existing DAISY/DocAccessible benchmark evidence. Until then, the question is viable, the broad framing is not, and the appropriate decision is **GO WITH SCOPE CHANGES**.
