# Corpus Limitation Note

The 13 atomic fixtures are intentionally controlled, not statistically representative of all real documents. Each isolates a target accessibility property, improving causal interpretation and source-ground-truth verification. The same design limits external validity: feature families have unequal complexity, most have one primary instance, and integrated real-world documents were not part of the frozen benchmark. Broader variants and integrated documents are appropriate future work, not missing evidence to be silently inferred here.
