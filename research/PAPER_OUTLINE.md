# Paper Outline

1. **Introduction:** accessible source does not guarantee preservation through conversion; gap, questions, and contributions.
2. **Background and related work:** preservation requirements, conversion/remediation work, PDF evaluation, and distinction from destination-only checking.
3. **A11yPreserve:** source contracts, frozen corpus, ASIR, destination extraction, and `a11ydiff`.
4. **Experimental method:** 13 fixtures, two pipelines, provenance, validation, classifications, and denominator rules.
5. **Results:** overall accounting, feature-level matrix, confirmed losses, cross-pipeline differences, and measurement error.
6. **Discussion:** what source-grounded measurement adds, pipeline attribution, practical interpretation, and contribution boundary.
7. **Threats to validity:** synthetic corpus, cloud versioning, representation equivalence, parser limits, scope, and absence of user study.
8. **Conclusion:** bounded findings and next steps.
