# Anonymization report

This directory is the double-blind ICST 2027 submission copy.

- The author block is `Anonymous for review`; the only acknowledgment is the neutral IEEE AI disclosure and it contains no affiliation, personal URL, repository URL, or identifying grant text.
- The manuscript uses neutral prior-work wording and does not identify an author-owned repository.
- Local filesystem paths and machine identities are absent from the submission source and PDF.
- The companion artifact is a sanitized anonymous candidate. It contains no browser profile, cookies, OAuth state, token, credential, or private URL.
- The canonical manuscript, frozen evidence, and internal reports remain outside this submission copy and were not modified to achieve anonymity.
- Final author metadata must be inserted by the researcher only after choosing the venue and before submission.

The title and scientific content are retained because ICST requires anonymous authorship, not anonymization of the research question or result labels.
