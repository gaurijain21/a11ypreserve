\section{V6 Scientific Hardening}
\label{sec:v6-hardening}

V6 is additive to the frozen V4 primary experiment and the historical V5 hardening layer. It does not rewrite V4/V5 classifications, fixtures, PDFs, manifests, or denominators. New empirical results are generated under \texttt{experiments/v6/}.

\subsection{No-op control correction}
The V5 destination-mutation implementation contained a circular control construction: it copied the baseline classification into the control field and assigned the false-positive flag without rerunning the baseline PDF. V6 removed that implementation flaw. For each of the 65 unmutated LibreOffice baseline PDFs, the harness reopened the actual PDF, reran source and destination extraction, reran the frozen comparator and secondary evidence path, computed a fresh classification, and compared normalized semantic evidence with the archived extraction.

All 65 controls were evaluated. The fresh classification disagreed with the frozen classification in 0/65 cases; extraction failed in 0/65 cases; and normalized semantic evidence differed in 0/65 cases. The measured control disagreement rate is therefore 0/65 (0.0\%). This is a genuine rerun result, not the historical V5 hard-coded control outcome. Per-case hashes, timestamps, tool versions, classifications, and evidence hashes are retained in \texttt{experiments/v6/control\_rerun\_cases/}.

\subsection{Destination mutation sensitivity}
V6 separates mutation application, PDF validity, evidence-level change, contracted-semantics change, classification-level detection, and meaningful survival. Across 65 attempts, 59 mutations were applied and produced valid PDFs, 46 changed the coarse classification, and 10 changed normalized relevant evidence and the contracted-semantics signature without changing the coarse classification. Six attempts were unsupported: five decorative-image operators found no actual \texttt{/Artifact} node and one hyperlink operator found no URI action. No mutant was invalid or inconclusive. The 10 meaningful survivors are retained as comparator blind spots, not relabeled as detections. Family-level results are reported in the replication package because the pooled 46/59 ratio hides substantial heterogeneity.

As an additive diagnostic layer, V6 also raises an evidence-drift alert whenever a valid applied mutant changes normalized extracted evidence, regardless of whether the six-way label changes. This alert fires for 59/59 valid mutants, including the 10 meaningful survivors and three additional unchanged-classification evidence changes; it does not rewrite the frozen six-way sensitivity result.

\subsection{Baselines and ablations}
The V6 baseline study executes six methods on the same 130 primary atomic cases and evaluates fault sensitivity on the 59 known valid applied mutants. No independent adjudicated reference labels were available for the primary cases; consequently V6 reports observed decisions and abstentions rather than false-preservation or false-loss rates. On the known-fault arm, PDF tag presence detected 0/59 faults, visible-text equality detected 0/59, destination-only feature evidence detected 16/59, and the full source-grounded method detected 46/59. The no-secondary ablation also detected 46/59 but changed difficult primary outcomes: it converted some abstentions into confident losses. The no-abstention role-presence policy detected 16/59 and forced every primary case into a binary decision.

\subsection{Interpretation boundaries and chronology}
The Google condition is an end-to-end DOCX import, internal Google representation, and PDF-download pipeline. V6 makes no stage-level causal claim. The LibreOffice profile-B comparison remains a within-LibreOffice robustness check: 65/65 outputs were valid and the frozen classifier changed in 0 cases. The V4 primary denominator remains 130; D/E contributes 52 within-design holdout observations and A--C contributes 78 non-holdout observations; Word remains a supplementary 65-observation arm.

The retained repeatability records show classification stability for 6/6 targeted variants across three runs per pipeline, but the historical top-level repeatability manifest still says \texttt{NOT\_RUN}. V6 treats this as a documentation-integrity issue and does not use the repeated runs as time-separated Google evidence. A blinded 26-case adjudication packet and rubric are prepared but not executed; no standards-validator, rights-cleared natural corpus, external expert adjudication, or human usability result is claimed.

\subsection{V6 contribution boundary}
V6 strengthens the testing contribution rather than converting A11yPreserve into a general PDF checker. It shows that an apparently perfect no-op-control headline can be invalid if controls are not independently executed; it quantifies where simple destination checks fail on known destination faults; and it makes semantically meaningful classification survivors visible. The remaining claim is bounded: source-grounded contracts, multiple evidence paths, and abstention support auditable preservation testing for selected machine-observable semantics under recorded conversion conditions.
