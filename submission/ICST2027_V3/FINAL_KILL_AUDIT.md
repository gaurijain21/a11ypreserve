# TOP 5 MOST PLAUSIBLE REJECTION REASONS

1. The 13 one-per-feature fixtures and two pipelines may be judged too narrow for a testing-track evaluation.
2. The contribution may be seen as a DOCX-specific harness adjacent to DocAccessible, DAISY, and destination-PDF benchmarking rather than a sufficiently novel testing contribution.
3. Six unresolved primary cases and the calibration false negatives may be interpreted as inadequate oracle power.
4. The suite has a documented selection rationale but no formal adequacy metric or complete fault model.
5. Reusability is packaged and documented but has no independent external execution and no general adapter API.

## Final audit judgment

These are real reviewer risks, but none invalidates the bounded claims. They do not justify changing frozen classifications or adding an experiment at this stage. No fatal methodological issue was found, and no new experiment is required to make the claims defensible.
