## Determination

Disclosure is mandatory for this project. Editing/grammar-only use is described by IEEE as generally outside the intended mandatory disclosure, although disclosure is recommended; the documented use here also included drafting/restructuring, related-work organization, adversarial review, and artifact/package documentation. The manuscript therefore identifies OpenAI ChatGPT and OpenAI Codex, names the affected sections, and describes the assistance briefly.

The current official IEEE/ICST sources reviewed require acknowledgment disclosure and section-level explanation but do not require a separate bibliography entry for ChatGPT or Codex. No unverifiable model version, date, or AI-system DOI was invented. The author should recheck any portal-specific field immediately before upload.

## Actual use boundary

The systems assisted with manuscript prose and organization, related-work organization, methodological/adversarial review, verification-script support, and submission-artifact documentation and packaging. They were not used as experimental ground truth, independent human validation, or autonomous decision-makers for frozen classifications or final scientific conclusions. The authors verified AI-assisted material against source artifacts, frozen evidence, analysis records, and cited literature.

## Double-anonymity implication

The disclosure is neutral and contains no author names, institutions, account identifiers, repository URLs, local paths, or workflow details that identify the authors. Author metadata remains a human-controlled insertion at submission time.
