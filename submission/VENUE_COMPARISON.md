## Interpretation

ICST is the best currently actionable target because the frozen paper's central contribution is a source-grounded testing and conformance protocol, not a population study or a user study. DocEng is the strongest topical match overall, but its current cycle is closed and the next call is not yet available. ASSETS is a credible later-cycle option, with a higher risk that reviewers will expect direct user validation. SANER Research is closed; SANER RENE is still open but would require reframing the paper around reproducibility/negative results. ISSRE is methodologically adjacent but less naturally aligned with document transformation.
