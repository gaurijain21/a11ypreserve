# COMPILES WITH NON-BLOCKING WARNINGS

The revised manuscript compiles successfully with MiKTeX pdfTeX 25.12.0.0 and BibTeX 0.99e after the title, methodology, and introduction edits. The final build requires the normal BibTeX plus two LaTeX passes and produces an 8-page, 214,499-byte PDF.

The Codex built-in compiler remains unavailable because it fails before source parsing with `Unable to find standard directories for platform`. The documented local fallback succeeds. Remaining warnings are underfull boxes in narrow two-column text and MiKTeX's update-check notice; there are no undefined citations, undefined references, overfull boxes, malformed bibliography errors, or clipped content in the rendered inspection.
