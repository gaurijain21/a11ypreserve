## Decision

The paper survives hostile review at its stated scope. The exact final decision is **READY FOR SUBMISSION**.

## Strongest valid rejection argument

The strongest objection is limited external validity: several historical PARTIALLY_PRESERVED cases are unresolved DOCX-to-PDF equivalence rather than definite degradation, the atomic corpus has one fixture per feature family, and the Google Docs condition is a dated cloud pipeline whose backend cannot be independently pinned.

## Is the objection fatal?

No. The confirmed F06/Google Docs and F11/Google Docs losses have independent raw-PDF and text-extraction support. F11/LibreOffice remains MEASUREMENT_ERROR. The final V2 model reports 11 VERIFIED_PRESERVED, 3 OBSERVED_PARTIAL, 3 ALTERED, 2 CONFIRMED_LOST, 6 UNRESOLVED_EQUIVALENCE, and 1 MEASUREMENT_ERROR. The three integrated documents provide a secondary applicability check without inflating the primary denominator.
