# Venue-Fit Analysis

## Primary positioning

The strongest intellectual positioning is an **accessibility measurement paper with document-engineering evidence**. The core contribution is the source-grounded preservation question; document engineering supplies the format and pipeline rigor. Software testing is a valuable secondary lens through the constructed oracle and differential comparison, but the current corpus is too small for a broad testing-systems claim.
