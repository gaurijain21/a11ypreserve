# V4 final upload decision

Decision: **GO WITH LIMITATIONS**.

## Why GO

- The final manuscript is an 8-page anonymous IEEEtran conference-format full paper on letter paper, below the 10-page ICST Research Papers limit.
- Two legitimate primary pipelines are complete: 65 atomic outputs each for LibreOffice and Google Docs, analyzed as 130 cases without changing frozen rules.
- The paper reports feature-level evidence, holdout, calibration, source-oracle mutation testing, realistic/integrated documents, repeatability, related work, threats, and AI disclosure.
- The final PDF is parseable, unencrypted, visually reviewed, and free of unresolved citations/references and overfull boxes.
- The anonymous upload bundle and sanitized replication package are hashed and ready.

## Limitations to disclose

- Microsoft Word desktop native GUI export was recovered and evaluated as a separately frozen supplementary third pipeline. COM remains limited by 0x80070520, but all 65 Word atomic and 10 realistic PDFs are valid, nonzero, parseable, and tagged.
- Google Docs is a dated cloud condition and requires an authorized session for reproduction.
- The atomic fixtures are controlled and purposive; the ten-document realistic arm is project-created/integrated, not a natural external corpus.
- No disabled-user study, complete PDF accessibility assessment, prevalence estimate, global converter ranking, defensible reading-order oracle, or complete optional validator arm is claimed.
- Word was added after primary-result completion and does not change the 130-case primary denominator.
- HotCRP author/COI metadata, final simultaneous-submission confirmation, and any portal-specific artifact access/license settings remain author-controlled checks.
