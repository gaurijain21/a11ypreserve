## Adversarial conclusion

The general ideas of source contracts, differential testing, metamorphic
relations, and mutation testing are established. The defensible distinction is
the focused DOCX-to-PDF preservation setting, the feature-family contract
matrix, the evidence-aware six-way outcome boundary, the archived controlled
benchmark, and the explicit separation of destination accessibility from
source-semantic preservation. The work should not claim novelty for inventing
testing oracles, metamorphic testing, PDF tagging checks, or PDF/UA validation.

The V7 results strengthen the empirical contribution but do not establish a
new general testing theory. Prior-art review remains a human reference-check
item before submission.
