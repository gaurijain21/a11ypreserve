## Full-paper assessment

The earlier V4 PDF was a three-page single-column draft. The CFP does not state a formal minimum page count, so three pages was not treated as a literal page-limit violation. It was nevertheless inadequate as a full research paper for this evidence: the controlled corpus, source-contract method, cross-format taxonomy, holdout, calibration, source-oracle mutation testing, realistic arm, repeatability, related work, and threats could not be presented with sufficient methodological detail.

The final manuscript is an eight-page IEEE conference paper. It contains the requested introduction, related work, method, experimental design, bounded research questions, feature-level and aggregate results, holdout/calibration/repeatability/realistic-arm reporting, discussion, threats to validity, conclusion, supplementary Word disposition, and anonymous AI disclosure. It remains below the 10-page content limit without compressed geometry or reference-only overflow. On content and format, it now qualifies as a plausible full-paper submission; acceptance remains a reviewer decision.
