# V4 AI-disclosure audit

Audit date: 2026-10-04. The official ICST CFP was checked for its generative-AI disclosure requirement. The final anonymous manuscript includes an `AI DISCLOSURE` section immediately before the references.

The disclosure identifies OpenAI ChatGPT and OpenAI Codex; identifies affected sections (Introduction, Background and Related Work, Method, Experimental Design, Discussion, Threats to Validity, Conclusion), verification-script and submission-artifact/package documentation; and describes the extent as drafting, restructuring, adversarial manuscript review, verification-script work, and packaging assistance.

It also states that the systems were not experimental ground truth, independent human validation, or autonomous decision-makers for frozen classifications or final scientific conclusions, and that human authors reviewed the source artifacts, frozen evidence, analysis records, and cited literature.

The disclosure contains no author names, institutions, account identifiers, or acknowledgments that would defeat double-anonymous review. It does not claim AI-generated experimental evidence.
