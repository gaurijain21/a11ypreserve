## What the retained evidence supports

Six selected variants were converted three times per pipeline from identical
source documents. The retained records show classification stability and
normalized semantic-evidence stability for all six variants across LibreOffice,
Google Docs, and Word under the recorded sessions. Word output bytes were not
identical for all selected variants, so byte identity is not claimed.

## What it does not support

These runs were not two Google Docs sessions separated by at least seven days,
were not preregistered temporal repeatability, and do not establish service
timelessness or cloud-version invariance. V6 therefore reports them as a
targeted operational repeatability probe and documentation-integrity evidence,
not as an independent temporal experiment.
