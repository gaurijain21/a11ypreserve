# Scientific release verdict

Verdict: GO WITH LIMITATIONS.

Completed and verified: independent no-copy mutation ground truth, per-case
hashes and traces, family-level sensitivity, conservative benign controls,
archived cross-converter mutation diagnostics, fixture coverage audit, and an
offline V8 reproduction entry point. Historical V4/V5/V6 evidence and
denominators remain unchanged.

Limitations that remain material: no independent human/adjudicated ground
truth; controlled synthetic fixtures and a within-design holdout; 13 direct
mechanical mutation faults not detected by the coarse classifier; no
rights-cleared natural corpus; no temporal Google repeatability; no standards
validator run; and no human usability study.

The package is technically suitable as a transparent submission candidate only
with these limitations disclosed. Author-controlled declarations, final
conference compliance, licensing, and submission authorization remain outside
automated verification.
