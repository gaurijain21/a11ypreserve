# Adversarial review after the V8 audit

## Reviewer A: software testing

The strongest repair is the non-circular Tier-A mutation oracle. It verifies
59/59 applied transitions and separates 46 coarse detections from 13 direct
survivors. The remaining objection is that the oracle is still a bounded
mechanical reference, not a complete semantic oracle; mutation operators also
remain concentrated on one object per fixture.

## Reviewer B: accessibility

Raw role, alternative-text, language, URI, and metadata transitions are useful
machine-observable evidence, but do not establish PDF/UA conformance, reading
order, alternate representations, or human usability. The package now says so
explicitly. PAC/veraPDF and user studies remain unavailable/not executed.

## Reviewer C: experimental methodology

The five variants per family are correlated; the holdout is within-design;
Google Docs is an end-to-end import/export condition; Word is supplementary;
and no external adjudicated labels exist. These are now explicit boundaries.
The cross-converter mutation audit is descriptive because it reuses archived
outputs and does not localize pipeline stages.

Overall status: the circular-control objection is repaired for the bounded
mutation question. The external-validity and human-ground-truth objections are
mitigated only by transparent scope limitation, not eliminated.
