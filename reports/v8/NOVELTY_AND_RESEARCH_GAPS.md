# Novelty and research gaps

Status: COMPLETED WITH LIMITATIONS.

The defensible contribution is a source-grounded preservation-testing workflow
that treats conversion as a contract-preservation problem, collects multiple
destination evidence paths, and retains abstention/partial outcomes instead
of forcing unsupported binary claims. The new audit strengthens that claim by
showing that direct injected contract faults can be independently established
without copying the comparator's expected classification, while also exposing
13 coarse-classification blind spots.

The contribution is not a general PDF accessibility checker, PDF/UA validator,
human usability study, converter ranking, natural-corpus generalization, or
independent expert adjudication. The strongest remaining limitations are the
mechanical rather than human reference oracle, controlled fixture external
validity, and surviving faults in complex tables, captions, equations,
footnotes, lists, and hyperlinks.
