# V4 Validator Comparison

## Decision

The optional destination-validator arm was omitted. No complete, automatable PAC, Acrobat Accessibility Checker, or veraPDF installation was available in the environment, and the available `pdfinfo`/PyMuPDF tools are evidence extractors rather than accessibility validators.

The primary V4 question remains source-grounded preservation. Destination-only validator output would answer a different question and was not invented from incomplete tooling. The omission is an accepted scope limitation, not a claim that the PDFs passed or failed external conformance certification.
