# Limitations

- Two tested pipeline conditions do not represent the converter ecosystem.
- Google import/export is an end-to-end opaque cloud workflow; the backend is not fully pinned.
- The 13 feature families are purposive and measurability-biased, with two controlled instances each; coverage is not comprehensive.
- Eighteen atomic rows remain unresolved equivalence, and calibration contains two false negatives plus seven other unresolved/indeterminate controls.
- Word was not added because COM activation failed; reading order was deferred because no reliable automated contract was established.
- The integrated documents are project-generated, not a representative real-world corpus.
- No disabled-user study or screen-reader task study was conducted.
- The initial Core evidence was not collected under the final taxonomy; the taxonomy evolution and reclassification procedure are disclosed.

These limitations constrain the scope of inference but do not invalidate the narrower protocol contribution when claims remain bounded to the named fixtures, evidence, and conditions.
