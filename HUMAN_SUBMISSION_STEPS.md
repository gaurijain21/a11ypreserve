# Human submission steps

1. Open the ICST 2027 Research Paper submission portal.
2. Enter the final paper title exactly as frozen.
3. Enter author names, order, and affiliations.
4. Paste or check the abstract.
5. Select topics and keywords.
6. Enter conflicts if requested.
7. Complete originality and concurrent-submission declarations.
8. Complete AI/policy declarations if the portal asks separately.
9. Select copyright and license options.
10. Upload the exact frozen paper PDF: `submission/ICST2027/main.pdf`.
11. Upload or link the exact frozen artifact if requested: `submission/A11yPreserve_artifact_ICST2027.zip`.
12. Inspect the PDF generated or served by the portal.
13. Verify title, pages, figures, references, and anonymity state appropriate for review.
14. Approve submission.
15. Save the submission confirmation and ID.

Do not replace the frozen upload pair without a new audit. Do not automate the final Submit action.
