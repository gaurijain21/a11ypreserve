# Human submission steps

1. Inspect the final anonymized ICST PDF and candidate artifact.
2. Insert and confirm the final author metadata in the submission copy.
3. Create or sign in to the ICST 2027 HotCRP account.
4. Confirm the Research Papers track, title, authors, conflicts, and originality declarations.
5. Choose the final artifact license and update the anonymous citation metadata as appropriate.
6. Upload the paper and anonymized replication package.
7. Inspect the generated submission PDF and metadata in HotCRP.
8. Approve the final submission personally.
